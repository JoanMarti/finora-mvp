# Android release 1.0: Google Play, Switzerland

This is the checklist for the first Finora release on Google Play. What the repository already provides is marked ✅; what needs a person (accounts, legal texts, Play Console forms) is marked ☐.

## What version 1.0 is

Version 1.0 is an **early-access** build:

- Bank connections run with generated demo data. bLink and OpenWealth are not connected yet.
- Accounts are kept on the device, because the build has no `FINORA_API_URL`.
- No payment is taken. Premium (trial and subscription) is a demo and does not use Google Play Billing.
- Everything else works with the user's own figures: goals, budgets, assets, profile, simulators, calendar.

The store listing says this openly ("Early access"). Recommended path: internal testing, then closed testing, then production once the blockers at the end of this file are solved.

## 2. Upload key (once) ☐

1. Create the upload key and keep it in a password manager (losing it means asking Google to reset it):
   ```sh
   keytool -genkey -v -keystore finora-upload.jks -keyalg RSA -keysize 2048 \
     -validity 10000 -alias upload
   ```
2. For local builds, create `android/key.properties`. It is git-ignored; never commit it.
   ```properties
   storeFile=/absolute/path/finora-upload.jks
   storePassword=…
   keyAlias=upload
   keyPassword=…
   ```
3. For CI, add these repository secrets: `ANDROID_KEYSTORE_BASE64` (output of `base64 -w0 finora-upload.jks`), `ANDROID_KEYSTORE_PASSWORD`, `ANDROID_KEY_ALIAS` and `ANDROID_KEY_PASSWORD`. The `android` job then signs the bundle with the upload key.
4. In Play Console, accept **Play App Signing**: Google keeps the app signing key, and you only ever use the upload key.

## 3. Play Console account ☐

- **Account type:** organisation or personal.
  - An organisation account needs a D-U-N-S number for the legal entity. It is recommended for a financial app.
  - A **personal account created after 13.11.2023** must run a **closed test with at least 12 testers for 14 days in a row** before it can apply for production ([Google](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465)).
- **Identity:** developer identity verification and the one-time registration fee.
- **Contact details:** a public contact e-mail and website for the store listing.

## 6. Release tracks ☐

1. **Internal testing** (up to 100 testers, available in minutes): upload the signed `.aab` from CI.
2. **Closed testing**: at least 12 testers for 14 days on a personal account. Collect feedback in the form linked in the release notes.
3. **Production**, Switzerland only, after the blockers below.

## 7. Blockers before production ☐

- **Premium purchases:** Google Play requires Play Billing for digital subscriptions. Either implement `PurchaseService` with `in_app_purchase` (monthly CHF 6.90, yearly CHF 59, 14-day trial as an introductory offer), or hide the purchase and keep Premium free during early access.
- **Legal texts:** operator name and address, full terms of use, and a lawyer's review of the privacy policy, the consent texts (nLPD) and the offers model (LSFin; FINMA registration if Finora is paid directly by insurers).
- **Bank data:** connect bLink and OpenWealth through the backend, or keep the early-access label and the demo data clearly marked.
- **Translations:** a native review of the DE, FR and IT app and store texts.
- **Store screenshots:** French and Italian sets (add `fr-FR` and `it-IT` to the screenshot test).
- **Bank names in the demo:** the demo and the store screenshots show real Swiss bank names and colours. Before production, get written permission or switch the store screenshots to neutral demo institutions, so the listing does not suggest an affiliation (Google Play impersonation and IP policies).
