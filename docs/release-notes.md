**Finora — early access for Switzerland.** Your Swiss finances in one place, read-only.

### Install on Android
1. On your phone, download `finora-<version>.apk` below.
2. Open it and allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser when Android asks.
3. Open Finora and choose **Explore with demo data**, or create an account.

Android 7.0 or newer. This APK is installed outside Google Play: Premium can be tried with the free trial, but nothing is charged. The `-play.aab` file is the bundle for Google Play Console.

### Good to know
- Bank connections show generated demo data; bLink and OpenWealth are not connected yet.
- Your account and data stay on your phone.
- Web version: https://joanmarti.github.io/finora-mvp/
