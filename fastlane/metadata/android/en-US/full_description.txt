Finora brings your Swiss finances together in one clear view: accounts, savings, investments, Pillar 3a, mortgage and the bills that come back every month. Read-only by design: Finora never moves money and never asks for your e-banking password.

EARLY ACCESS
This first version is an early-access preview. Bank connections run with generated demo data while the bLink and OpenWealth connections are being finished. Everything else works with your own figures: goals, budgets, assets, the simulators and the financial calendar.

SEE EVERYTHING AT ONCE
• Net worth and its trend across all banks
• Spending analysis, recurring bills and subscriptions
• Monthly report: what you saved and where it went

PLAN AHEAD
• Savings goals and a wealth plan
• Retirement outlook across AHV/AVS, pension fund and Pillar 3a
• "Can I buy a home?" with the rules Swiss banks apply
• Tax savings from Pillar 3a and pension fund buy-ins
• Financial calendar: health insurance switch by 30 November, Pillar 3a by 31 December, tax return

SAVE MONEY
• Clear hints next to the bill or account they are about: health and household insurance, mobile plans, savings interest, Pillar 3a fees
• Every estimate explains what it is based on
• Offers only with your express consent; partner offers are labelled and never ranked by what a partner pays

PRIVACY FIRST
• Your profile, goals and settings stay on your phone
• No advertising, no tracking SDKs, no data sales
• PIN lock and a mode that hides all amounts

FREE AND PREMIUM
Free has no time limit and includes up to 3 banks, 6 months of history and 2 goals. Finora Premium adds unlimited banks and goals, 24 months of history, retirement scenarios, the household view and the tax export. During early access no payment is taken.

Finora gives information, not financial advice. Made in Switzerland, in English, German, French and Italian.
