Delete your Finora account

This page explains how to delete your account in the Finora app by Finora and the data linked to it.

In the app

  1. Open Finora and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account at the bottom and confirm.

The account is deleted on our servers and all data on the device is removed straight away.

Without the app

Write from the e-mail address of your account to [support e-mail address] with the subject "Delete my Finora account". We confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: first name, e-mail, password hash and sign-in tokens.
  • Consents, reminders and settings stored with your account.
  • Everything stored on your device: profile, goals, budgets, assets, settings and PIN.

What is kept

Records the law requires us to keep, such as receipts of Premium payments, for the legal period (10 years). Aggregated offer counts stored under a random installation id contain no personal data and are not linked to your account. A Google Play subscription must be cancelled in Google Play.
